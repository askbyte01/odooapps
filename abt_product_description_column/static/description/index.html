Separate Product Description Column in Odoo v18

Overview

  • Askbye Technoleb introduces the much-requested feature of separating product and description fields in Odoo v18, which was previously available in older versions of the system. This feature allows users to have distinct fields for product names and detailed descriptions, improving clarity and efficiency in various document views such as sales orders, invoices, and purchase orders.
  • The separation of product and description fields enhances user experience by making it easier to differentiate between product attributes and their detailed descriptions, ensuring a smoother workflow in Odoo. This feature is designed to simplify the process of generating and viewing documents by providing better organization and flexibility.
  • With this improvement, businesses can now provide more accurate and descriptive product details, which can be crucial for customer-facing documents like invoices and sales orders. It also makes it easier for sales teams and accounting staff to process orders and maintain accurate records.
  • This feature not only improves the interface but also enhances backend functionality, enabling more efficient customization and integrations with other systems. Whether you're working on quotes, orders, or invoices, the separation of product names and descriptions ensures that each field serves its unique purpose, making the entire process more streamlined and efficient.

Functionality Screenshots

  • Sales Order Form View (Before): The product name and description are combined into one field, which makes the document harder to read.
  • Sales Order Form View (After): Product name and description are separated, improving readability and clarity.
  • Invoice Form View (Before): The combined field for product name and description makes complex invoices harder to process.
  • Invoice Form View (After): Separated fields provide clarity and improved layout for invoices with multiple items.
  • Purchase Order Form View (Before): Merged fields make tracking purchase order items difficult.
  • Purchase Order Form View (After): Fields are now clearly separated, allowing for easy tracking and management of purchase orders.
